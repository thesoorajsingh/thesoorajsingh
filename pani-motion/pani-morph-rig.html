Baked, not solved

A build step matches every outline once — resampling each pair to a shared point count and rotating the index order until the features line up — then writes the result out as plain coordinate arrays.

What ships is 53 kB of numbers and about a hundred lines of drawing code. No morph library, no JSX transform, no React: the page loads one script and lerps.

Where the weight comes from

Six authored keyframes sit between the exports. A wind-up leans the glass −7° before it tips; foam lags the rotation, pools downhill, and is held inside the body outline so it can never splatter onto open blue; the eyes lift into their open seat as they thin, so one eye opens instead of two eyes swapping.

Between keyframes the rig runs a Catmull‑Rom spline in p, not a straight line — velocity carries through each knot instead of restarting at it.

One element

The mascot is self-contained. Drop it in, give it a state, and it owns its own spring:

<pani-mascot state="greeting"></pani-mascot>

el.goTo("pouring")

Add auto and it wires its own hover and click. Everything on this page is the same element at a different p.
